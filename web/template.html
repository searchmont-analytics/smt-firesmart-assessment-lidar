How the scores are built

Each house footprint is found in the point cloud from smooth, single-return roof surfaces. Rings are measured from the wall: 0–1.5 m, Zone 1 (1.5–10 m) and Zone 2 (10–30 m), following the FireSmart Home Ignition Zone.

Canopy is the share of each ring covered by vegetation 3 m or taller. Ladder fuel is the share of each ring with LiDAR returns between 1 and 4 m: shrubs, regen and low branches that carry fire from the ground into crowns.

Thresholds

Each measure scores 0 at the “ok” value and 100 at the “max” value. Zone 1 counts for 60% of the overall score, Zone 2 for 40%.

Flags add points: branches over the roof, an outbuilding within 10 m, or a steep slope.

What LiDAR can’t see

  • The house itself: vents, gutters, decks, siding and roofing are where embers get in. Only an on-site assessment checks these.
  • Fuel type. Conifers and dry grass burn very differently from birch and maple.
  • Change since the flight. Ontario tiles can be several years old.
